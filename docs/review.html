Blind hand-check: LLM vs keyword baseline

These are 50 posts drawn at random (seed 42) from the LLM-tagged set. For each post, open the original listing and choose the correct role and city yourself. Neither system's tags are shown while you label, so they can't influence you.

When all 50 are labeled, the page scores both the LLM and the keyword baseline against your labels. Labels are saved in this browser. When you're done, click Download results and commit the file as eval/hand_check.json.

Labeling rules (decided before labeling)
  • Role = what the person will spend most of their time doing, read from the listing's responsibilities, not from the job title or the company's industry.
  • Sales & Business Development: the job is selling, i.e. lead generation, calling, closing, field sales, relationship manager or pre-sales. This applies even when the title says "Marketing" but the duties are selling.
  • Marketing & Content: campaigns, SEO, social media, content writing, branding, video or graphics for marketing.
  • Operations & Customer Support: coordination, admin, back office, customer service, onboarding, personal or executive assistant.
  • Software Engineering: building software, including web, app, backend, full-stack and SDE roles. Data Science & AI/ML: data analysis, ML, AI, BI and analytics. DevOps, Cloud & IT: infrastructure, IT support and security.
  • Product & Design: product manager and UI/UX roles. Graphic design only counts here when it is product design; otherwise it is Marketing.
  • Use Other only when no category fits. If a role genuinely splits 50/50, pick the one the responsibilities list first.
  • City is the primary work location on the listing:
    • Use the standard spelling (Bengaluru, Gurugram, Delhi).
    • "Work from home" or remote means Remote.
    • Several cities with no main one means Multiple.
    • Only the country given means India.
